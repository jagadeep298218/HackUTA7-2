Enhanced Gaze Tracking Reading Test

Welcome to the gaze tracking reading demonstration. This system uses WebGazer to monitor your eye movements as you read through the text on this page.

When you focus your gaze on any paragraph for more than 500 milliseconds, the text will automatically enlarge to improve readability. This feature is especially helpful for students with reading difficulties or visual impairments.

The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog. This classic pangram contains every letter of the English alphabet and is commonly used for testing fonts and keyboard layouts. Try focusing your gaze on this paragraph to see the text enlarge.

Educational technology continues to evolve, providing innovative solutions for diverse learning needs. Eye tracking technology represents a significant advancement in accessibility, allowing systems to adapt to where students are looking in real-time.

This demonstration uses fixation detection logic to determine when you are actively reading a specific text block. The system tracks the duration of your gaze and applies visual enhancements only when needed, creating a more natural and comfortable reading experience.

Move your eyes around the page to test different paragraphs. Notice how the text size returns to normal when you look away and grows larger when you focus on a new section. This dynamic behavior helps guide your attention and reduces eye strain during extended reading sessions.
